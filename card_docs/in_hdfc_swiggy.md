# Swiggy HDFC Bank Credit Card

The **Swiggy HDFC Bank Credit Card** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **HDFC Bank** running on the **MASTERCARD** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹500.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹500.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹200,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 3.50% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** 4 domestic airport lounge visits per calendar year.
- **International Lounge:** None
- **Ancillary Benefits:** Complimentary 3-month Swiggy One membership on activation.
