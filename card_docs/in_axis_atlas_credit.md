# Axis Bank Atlas Credit Card

The **Axis Bank Atlas Credit Card** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **Axis Bank** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹5,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹5,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹1,500,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 3.50% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** Up to 18 visits/year (Tier-based via Silver/Gold/Platinum spends).
- **International Lounge:** Up to 12 visits/year (Tier-based via Silver/Gold/Platinum spends).
- **Ancillary Benefits:** Airport concierge and meet-and-greet discounts.
