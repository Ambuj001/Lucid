# AU Nomad Credit Card

The **AU Nomad Credit Card** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **AU Small Finance Bank** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹2,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹2,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹250,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 0.00% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited domestic airport lounge access across India.
- **International Lounge:** Up to 2 international lounge visits per quarter based on spends.
- **Ancillary Benefits:** 0% Currency Forex Markup Fee (primary driver for international travelers).
