# Axis Magnus Credit Card (Burgundy Private)

The **Axis Magnus Credit Card (Burgundy Private)** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **Axis Bank** running on the **MASTERCARD** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹30,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹30,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹2,500,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 1.50% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited domestic lounge entries for primary + 8 guest visits per calendar year.
- **International Lounge:** Unlimited international lounge entries for primary + 8 guest visits per calendar year.
- **Ancillary Benefits:** 24 complimentary golf rounds or lessons per year, BMS BOGO movie tickets up to Rs 500, dedicated 24/7 Burgundy Private concierge.
