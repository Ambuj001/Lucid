# SBI Card Prime

The **SBI Card Prime** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **State Bank of India** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹2,999.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹2,999.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹300,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 3.50% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** 8 domestic lounge visits per year (capped at 2 per quarter).
- **International Lounge:** 4 international lounge visits per year via Priority Pass.
- **Ancillary Benefits:** Trident Hotels Web Privilege Elite Tier membership, Rs 1000 Pizza Hut milestone voucher.
