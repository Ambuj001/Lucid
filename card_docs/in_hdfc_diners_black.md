# HDFC Diners Club Black Metal Edition

The **HDFC Diners Club Black Metal Edition** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **HDFC Bank** running on the **AMEX** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹10,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹10,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹800,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 2.00% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited complimentary domestic lounge access globally for Primary and Add-on cardholders.
- **International Lounge:** Unlimited complimentary international lounge access globally for Primary and Add-on cardholders.
- **Ancillary Benefits:** 6 complimentary Golf rounds per quarter globally, Annual complimentary memberships to Forbes Digital, Marriott Bonvoy, Swiggy One on milestones.
