# HDFC Regalia Gold Credit Card

The **HDFC Regalia Gold Credit Card** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **HDFC Bank** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹2,500.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹2,500.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹300,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 2.00% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** 12 domestic lounge visits per year (unlimited for self via PP once spend rules are cleared).
- **International Lounge:** 6 international lounge visits per year for self and add-on cardholders.
- **Ancillary Benefits:** Complimentary flight insurance cover up to Rs 1 Crore, Uber/ITC vouchers on quarterly spend milestones.
