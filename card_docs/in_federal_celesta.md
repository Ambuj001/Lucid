The **Federal Bank Celesta Credit Card** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **Federal Bank** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹300,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.
