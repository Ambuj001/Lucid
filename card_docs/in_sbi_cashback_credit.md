# SBI Cashback

The **SBI Cashback** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **State Bank of India** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹999.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹999.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹200,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Basic perks:** 1% fuel surcharge waiver at authorized retail fuel outlets.
- **Lounge Access:** 4 complimentary domestic lounge visits per year, subject to spend rules in the previous quarter.

### ⚠️ Crucial Terms and Marketing Gimmicks
- 1% cashback on offline/other transactions. Auto-credited to statement.
- 💰 5% cashback on online shopping. Monthly cashback cap: Rs.5,000.
- 🛒 5% cashback on online grocery apps (Blinkit, BigBasket). Cap Rs.5,000.
- 🚫 Utility bills earn zero cashback.
- 🚫 Insurance payments earn zero cashback.
- 🚫 Wallet load is completely devalued to 0% cashback.
- 🚫 Rent payments earn zero cashback.
- 🚫 Government tax & utility fee payments earn zero cashback.
