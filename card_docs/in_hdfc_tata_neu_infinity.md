# HDFC Tata Neu Infinity Credit Card

The **HDFC Tata Neu Infinity Credit Card** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **HDFC Bank** running on the **RUPAY** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹1,499.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹1,499.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹300,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 2.00% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** 8 domestic lounge visits per year.
- **International Lounge:** 4 international lounge visits per year via Priority Pass.
- **Ancillary Benefits:** 1% fuel surcharge waiver capped at Rs 250/cycle.
