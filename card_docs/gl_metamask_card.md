## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹0.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹0.00 + GST

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Linked Accounts:** Direct debit from savings/current bank accounts. Subject to daily withdrawal limits.
- **Lounge Access:** 1 complimentary domestic lounge access per calendar quarter subject to meeting spend guidelines.
