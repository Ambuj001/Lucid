# HDFC Infinia Metal

The **HDFC Infinia Metal** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **HDFC Bank** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹12,500.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹12,500.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹1,000,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Super-Premium Perks:** Complimentary luxury golf rounds, 24/7 dedicated concierge assistance, and metal form factor.
- **Lounge Access:** Unlimited complimentary domestic and international airport lounge visits for both primary and add-on cardholders.

### ⚠️ Crucial Terms and Marketing Gimmicks
- Base 3.33% yield (5 points per Rs.150 spent, 1 point = Rs.1.00 for SmartBuy Travel).
- SmartBuy flights: 5X points multiplier applies. Limit 15,000 points/month.
- SmartBuy hotels: 5X points multiplier applies. Limit 15,000 points/month.
- ⚠️ Capped: Utility point yield is capped at 2,000 reward points per calendar month.
- ⚠️ Capped: Insurance payments capped at 2,000 points per transaction/day.
- 🚫 Wallet top-up is completely excluded from rewards.
- 🚫 Rent payments earn zero reward points.
- 🚫 Government taxes & public fund payments earn zero reward points.
- 🚫 Fuel spends excluded from points; 1% surcharge waiver applies up to Rs. 1,000/month.
