# Yes Marquee

The **Yes Marquee** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **Yes Bank** running on the **MASTERCARD** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹9,999.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹4,999.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹800,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Premium Perks:** Discounted dining programs, airline voucher milestones, and priority check-in benefits.
- **Lounge Access:** 8 to 12 complimentary domestic and 6 international lounge visits per calendar year.
