# Yes Bank Marquee Credit Card

The **Yes Bank Marquee Credit Card** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **Yes Bank** running on the **MASTERCARD** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹9,999.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹4,999.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹1,000,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 1.75% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** Unlimited domestic lounge visits for Primary, Add-on, and 8 complimentary guest visits per year.
- **International Lounge:** Unlimited international lounge visits for Primary, Add-on, and 8 complimentary guest visits per year.
- **Ancillary Benefits:** BOGO movie tickets on BMS (up to 3 free tickets/month, max Rs 250 each), 4 complimentary golf rounds/lessons per year.
