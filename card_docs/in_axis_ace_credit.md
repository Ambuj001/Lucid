# Axis Bank Ace Credit Card

The **Axis Bank Ace Credit Card** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **Axis Bank** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹499.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹499.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹200,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 3.50% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** 4 domestic lounge visits per year.
- **International Lounge:** None
- **Ancillary Benefits:** 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs 400 and Rs 4000 (capped at Rs 400/month).
