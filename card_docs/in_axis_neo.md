# Axis Neo

The **Axis Neo** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **Axis Bank** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹250.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹250.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹100,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Basic perks:** 1% fuel surcharge waiver at authorized retail fuel outlets.
- **Lounge Access:** 4 complimentary domestic lounge visits per year, subject to spend rules in the previous quarter.
