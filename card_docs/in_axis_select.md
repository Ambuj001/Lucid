# Axis Bank Select Credit Card

The **Axis Bank Select Credit Card** is a premium **Credit** card issued by **Axis Bank** running on the **VISA** network. Below are the verified fee structures, reward policies, exclusions, and card terms.

## 💳 Fee and Waiver Structure
- **Joining Fee:** ₹3,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee:** ₹3,000.00 + GST
- **Annual Fee Spend Waiver Threshold:** Spends exceeding ₹600,000.00 in the previous card anniversary year waive the subsequent year's fee.

## ✈️ Perks and Lounge Access
- **Forex Markup:** 3.50% + GST
- **Domestic Lounge:** 2 domestic lounge visits per quarter (requires Rs 50k spend in previous cycle).
- **International Lounge:** 6 international lounge visits per year via Priority Pass (requires Rs 50k spend in previous cycle).
- **Ancillary Benefits:** BOGO movie tickets on BMS up to Rs 300/month, Axis Dining Delights discounts.
